Privacy Policy

Lulu Follower Goal Twitch Extension

HomeTerms of Use

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Data Used by the Extension

The Extension uses information supplied through Twitch's Extension and API services that is necessary for its functionality. This may include the broadcaster's channel identifier, channel login, and the channel's total follower count.

2. No Developer Database

The current version of Lulu Follower Goal does not operate a separate developer database and does not intentionally store viewer names, email addresses, passwords, payment details, IP addresses, or individual follower identities.

3. Follow Action

When a viewer chooses to follow the channel, the Extension opens Twitch's native Follow confirmation interface. The follow action and notification preferences are handled by Twitch, not by Lulu Follower Goal.

4. Cookies and Analytics

The current version of the Extension does not use its own analytics service, advertising trackers, or developer-set tracking cookies.

5. Data Sharing

The Extension does not sell personal data. Data processed by Twitch is governed by Twitch's own privacy practices and policies.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Extension's functionality or data practices change. The “Last updated” date will reflect the current version.

7. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the creator through the Twitch channel Pososonya.
